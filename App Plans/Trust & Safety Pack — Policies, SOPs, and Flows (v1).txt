Trust & Safety Pack — Policies, SOPs, and Flows (v1)
0) Purpose and scope
Protect users, maximise show?up reliability, and keep the marketplace clean. Applies to web MVP and native.
1) Safety principles
? User control: block, report, decline, unmatch available in one tap.

? Minimum data: no precise location; broad availability only.

? Fast, visible action: moderation timers and outcomes shown in?app.

? Evidence first: decisions logged with artifacts and reviewer id.

? Proportionality: laddered enforcement; permanent bans for severe harm.

2) User?facing policies (summaries)
Community Guidelines
? Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate, slurs.

? No minors (must be 18+). No sexualised content of minors. Zero tolerance.

? No illegal activity, scams, or solicitation for money.

? No explicit nudity in profile photos. No weapons imagery intended to intimidate.

? Honest intent. No fake profiles, catfishing, or impersonation.

? Consent for off?app moves. Share contacts only after confirm.

Safety features
? Block (instant, silent). Unmatch (final). Report (categorized with evidence upload).

? Safety center with resources, date checklists, and reporting flow.

? Visible moderation SLA timer for each report.

Verification policy (MVP ? later)
? MVP: photo verification + pose prompt + face consistency check.

? Triggers for re?verification: burst reports, device change, profile image swap, reopen spam, show?rate anomaly.

? Later: liveness (video) and periodic re?checks.

3) Moderation SOP
Intake channels
? In?app report, email support, automated flags (keyword/URL, velocity, similarity).

Severity classes and SLAs
? S0 Critical (CSAM, credible threats, doxxing, coercion): action ? 1h; immediate quarantine; law?enforcement escalation.

? S1 High (fraud, impersonation, hate/harassment with threat, adult?service solicitations, stalking): action ? 12h.

? S2 Medium (spam, scams/links, ban evasion, obscene content without minors, repeated ghosting patterns): action ? 24h.

? S3 Low (profile quality violations, light rudeness, low?effort spam): action ? 48h.

Workflow
1. Create case with reporter id, accused id, type, evidence, timestamps.

2. Auto?mute messaging between parties for S0/S1 during review.

3. Review artifacts; run quick searches (linked devices, prior cases).

4. Decide action from Enforcement Matrix (below).

5. Notify reporter (status + SLA timer), and accused (where appropriate) with policy citation.

6. Log decision, reviewer, rationale, and evidence hashes. Close case.

Appeals
? 7?day window. Single appeal per action. New evidence required. Different reviewer. Decisions logged.

? Keyword/URL filters: scam terms, crypto, payment handles, contact handles; remove or hold.

? Image checks: NSFW heuristic, face consistency (profile vs selfie), duplicate image hash.

? Velocity/behavior: like/proposal/message bursts, identical notes, sub?minute intervals ? throttle + liveness challenge.

6) Anti?ghosting and reliability
? 72h proposal expiry; harsh turn?based penalties after first action.

? Inbound neglect penalties applied at 24h/48h.

? Reopen is receiver?paid, non?refundable, requires 2–3 times.

? Reliability gates boosts (Fast Pass, queue lift) and paid visibility features.

7) Transparency features
? Report status widget: New ? Under review ? Actioned/No action; SLA countdown visible.

? Case outcomes feed (anonymized aggregates): removals, bans, average SLA.

? Safety policy and enforcement page, updated monthly.

8) Data handling for safety
? Legal basis: legitimate interests for safety; consent for comms.

? Retention: reports and enforcement logs 24 months; CSAM evidence retained per law; audit logs 24 months.

? Access controls: least privilege; reviewer actions logged; dual control for S0.

? PII minimization: redact non?essential content in exports; blur images in internal tools by default.

9) Incident response runbook (abridged)
? CSAM/minors: lock account, preserve evidence, escalate to hotline/law enforcement; do not view more than necessary; manager approval required.

? Threats/stalking: collect logs, block, inform reporter with safety steps; escalate if imminent.

? Fraud rings: device ban, cluster queries, refund lock, notify payment provider if needed.

? Platform outage impacting safety: post status, pause paid flows, extend expiries by 24h.

10) Roles, permissions, tooling
? Reviewer: triage, enforce S2–S3, propose S1.

? Senior reviewer: enforce S1, approve device bans, handle appeals.

? Manager: S0 approvals, law?enforcement liaison.

? Tooling: mod console with queues, filters, evidence viewer, action templates, SLA timers, audit trail.

11) Safety copy (paste?ready)
? Report categories: Spam/Scam · Fake/Impersonation · Harassment/Hate · Threat/Coercion · Nudity · Minor · Off?platform solicitation · Other.

? Reporter receipts: “We’re reviewing your report. Typical response: 24h. You’ll see updates here.”

? Accused notice (non?severe): “We removed content that violates our Guidelines. Next time may lead to suspension.”

? Ban notice: “Your account violated our Guidelines. You may appeal within 7 days.”

12) KPIs and thresholds
? Moderation SLA hit?rate by severity. Target: S0 100%, S1 95%, S2 90%, S3 85%.

? Report rate per 1k sessions; unique reporter rate.

? False?positive rate on automation < 5% (spot?checks).

? Reliability distribution; no?show rate; reopen?abuse rate.

? Time to first moderator touch.

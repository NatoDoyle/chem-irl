3) Release process
1. Branch, PR, review checklist.

2. Staging deploy; Playwright E2E; manual UAT on mobile Safari + Chrome.

3. Feature flag default off.

4. Cut release Friday 16:00; deploy; enable to 5% Monday morning.

5. Watch guardrails 2h; then 25% ? 50% ? 100% per Data Pack.

6. Write release notes (internal + public changelog).

Hotfix path: patch branch, targeted deploy, post?mortem within 48h.

Supported environments

5) Bug/incident triage
Severity
? S0: data breach, auth broken, purchases failing globally ? stop?the?world, hotfix, status page.

? S1: critical flow broken (propose/confirm/chat) for many ? hotfix within 2h.

? S2: feature degraded, city?limited ? fix next release.

? S3: cosmetic or low?impact ? backlog.

Intake
? One queue (GitHub Issues or Linear). Templates for bug, incident, request.

? Minimum fields: env, steps, expected/actual, screenshots, logs, user_id if consented.

SLA
? S0 acknowledge ?15m; S1 ?30m; S2 ?24h; S3 ?72h.

Post?mortem (S0/S1)

6) Copy & content ops
Tone: clear, neutral, outcome?driven. No fluff.

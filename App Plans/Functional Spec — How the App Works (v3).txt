Hidden scoring systems
1) Action Speed Score (priority)
? Purpose: reward fast movement from match to confirmed time. Primary ranking key.

? Scale: 0–100. Not shown numerically. Inactivity floor 50. Below 50 only from inbound neglect or expiries. No decay. Score changes only via the daily engine and event points below.

2) Profile Quality Score
? Purpose: reflect how attractive/clear the profile is based on match acceptance rate.

? Primary metric: Match Acceptance Rate (MAR) = matches formed ÷ (likes on you + your likes shown to others), Bayesian?smoothed with a prior.

? Stabilisation: sample?size buckets and priors to avoid volatility on low exposure. Recent updates weighted more in the last 14 days.

? Effects: secondary rank boost and pre?match exposure lift; acts as tiebreaker when Action Speed is similar.

3) Reliability Score (least important)
? Purpose: reflect showed?up behaviour and respectful coordination.

? Inputs: decayed show?rate, cancellation honesty (gave notice + rescheduled), bilateral “would meet again”, and unresolved safety reports (negative).

? Window & decay: rolling 30 days; 10?day half?life on shows; heavier decay on old no?shows.

? Effects: gate and light boost only. Cannot outweigh poor Action Speed or Profile Quality. Low Reliability suppresses paid lifts and Fast Pass.

Scoring scales & update rules
Scale: all internal scores are 0–100. Shown to users only as badges. Scores clamp to 0–100 and decay over time.

Profile Quality Score (0–100) (0–100)
Based on Bayesian?smoothed Match Acceptance Rate (MAR) over 60 days.
? Prior: alpha0=8, beta0=12 (40% prior acceptance).

? Exposures: likes on you displayed + your likes that were shown.

? Estimator: MAR_hat = (alpha0 + matches) / (alpha0 + beta0 + exposures). Score = round(100 * MAR_hat).

? Stabilisation: until ?30 exposures, the prior dominates. Last 14 days count double.

? Profile completion bonuses: verified +3; ?4 photos +2; prompts completed +2 (never above 100). Reports do not change this score; they affect Report Risk.

Tie?breakers applied only when all three are equal (or in the same tie bucket):
? Freshness: most recent meaningful activity (proposal, confirm) wins.

Report Risk: used as an exclusion (for severe) or a post?sort demotion; never a positive.

B) Traffic & conversion
? Derive WAU/MAU from signups × activation rate × retention.

? Events per user: likes ? matches ? proposals ? confirms ? shows.

C) Revenue
? Subscriptions:

? New subs = MAU × payer_share_new

? Churn = prior_subs × churn_rate

? MRR_subs = active_subs × 14.99 / (1+VAT)

? Credits:

? Credit revenue = credits_sold × avg_price_per_credit / (1+VAT)

? Recognised revenue = min(consumed, sold + prior_deferred) × price; rest ? deferred

? Other: bonds forfeited (rare), fees.

D) Costs
? COGS: payment fees, creator CPA, verification API, SMS/email overages, app store fees (later).

? OPEX fixed: infra, tools, accounting, legal, insurance later.

? Payroll: founder salary from trigger month; contractors.

E) P&L and Cash
? P&L: Revenue – COGS – OPEX – Payroll = EBITDA.

? Cash: Opening + cash_from_sales ? fees ? refunds ? OPEX ? Payroll ? CAPEX (RN build).

? Maintain cash buffer ? 1 month of next month burn.

F) VAT (EU OSS)
? Compute VAT due = VAT_inclusive_revenue × VAT_rate / (1+VAT_rate) per country.

? File quarterly; set aside cash monthly.

8) Controls & fraud
? Chargebacks: track by cohort; auto?suspend on dispute; evidence via usage logs; cap reopen refunds.

? Credit abuse: throttle reopens with low confirm%, pause ability for 7 days; no refunds.

? Promo abuse: device/IP limits; referral requires bilateral “went.”

? Creator fraud: pay only on confirmed dates via their link; cap €/creator/month.

NewSubs_m = MAU_m * PayerRateNew_m
ChurnedSubs_m = ActiveSubs_{m-1} * Churn_m
ActiveSubs_m = ActiveSubs_{m-1} + NewSubs_m - ChurnedSubs_m

# Credits

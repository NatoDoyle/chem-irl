? COMPANY LEGAL NAME, LTD is the controller for your personal data.

? Contact: privacy@DOMAIN; postal address above. DPO (if appointed): NAME, CONTACT.

2. What we collect
? Account: email, phone (optional), age, gender, orientation, preferences, availability bands, profile prompts, photos, verification selfies.

? Usage: device, IP, logs, events (likes, matches, proposals, confirms, reopens, messages, surveys), payments (via Stripe tokens; we do not store full card data).

? Safety: reports, blocks, enforcement actions, signals (keyword/URL flags, velocity, duplicates).

? Optional: location at city/region granularity (no precise GPS), voice snippets if enabled.

3. Why we process & legal bases
? Provide the service (create account, discovery, proposals, chat after confirm): Art. 6(1)(b) contract.

? Payments (subscriptions/credits): contract; legal obligations for tax records.

? Safety & moderation (block/report, fraud/scam detection, reopen abuse checks): legitimate interests Art. 6(1)(f); we run a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA).

? Communications (transactional emails/SMS like confirmations, reminders): contract; marketing emails: consent Art. 6(1)(a).

? Analytics & A/B tests (product improvement): legitimate interests; we minimise and aggregate where possible.

? Legal compliance (record?keeping, responding to lawful requests): legal obligation Art. 6(1)(c).

? Automated ranking/scoring (Action Speed, Profile Quality, Reliability): legitimate interests; no legal or similarly significant effects. You may contact us to contest or request human review of decisions that materially affect you.

3. Risks: stalking, impersonation, image misuse, profiling fairness, data breach, minors lying about age.

4. Measures: verification, RLS, encryption, report tools, moderations SLAs, rate limits, transparent ranking, age gate, staff training.

5. Residual risk & decision: acceptable with controls; review with DPO/legal before public launch.

6. Consultation: if high risk remains, consult the DPC.

7. Review: repeat DPIA on native app launch and new features (voice, liveness).
